# Pickzella Pinterest Strategy — Pinning Playbook

> Goal: turn 130–150k monthly Pinterest views into blog traffic, email-free affiliate revenue, and AdSense impressions.

---

## 1. Pin specs (non-negotiable)

| Element | Spec |
|---|---|
| Pin size | **1000 × 1500 px (2:3 ratio)** — Pinterest's preferred vertical format |
| File | JPG under 1 MB (or PNG); keep text sharp |
| Title | Up to 100 chars — front-load the keyword (first 40 chars show in feed) |
| Description | Up to 500 chars — 1–2 sentences + keywords + CTA; first 50 chars matter most |
| Destination | Always the **blog article URL** (never just Amazon — Pinterest + Amazon links are fragile) |
| Text overlay | Big, readable at thumbnail size; max ~6 words on the image |

**Design formula that works for product pins:**
1. Background: bright lifestyle-ish color or product photo
2. Headline overlay: "Best Resistance Bands 2026" (matches article title)
3. Sub-line: "5 picks compared" / "Honest pros & cons"
4. Small brand mark: Pickzella logo/corner text

## 2. Board strategy (one board per category + intent boards)

| Board | Purpose | Example pins |
|---|---|---|
| Home Gym Essentials | Fitness articles | Resistance bands, dumbbells, jump ropes |
| Yoga & Recovery | Fitness/health | Yoga mats, foam rollers |
| Tech Under $50 | Tech gadgets | Earbuds, power banks, smart plugs |
| Home Office Setup | Tech/health | Desk lamps, posture correctors |
| Small Kitchen Wins | Home & kitchen | Air fryer, cookware, kettles |
| Clean Home, Happy Home | Home | Robot vacuums |
| Sleep Better Tonight | Health | Sleep masks |
| Hydration Station | Health | Water bottles |

**Rules:**
- Board titles = searchable keywords (not cute names). "Home Gym Essentials" beats "Sweat It 💦".
- Board descriptions: 1–2 sentences with keywords + what pinners will find.
- 20+ pins per board before judging performance; seed each board with your article pins + 5–10 curated repins.

## 3. Pinning frequency & scheduling

- **5–10 pins/day** to start (mix of new + repins of your own best pins to new boards).
- **80% your content / 20% curated** — Pinterest rewards active pinners, not just self-promoters.
- Use the native **Pinterest scheduler** (free, in-app): queue a week at a time, spread across the day.
- **Fresh pins > repins:** for each article, create **3–5 visually different pins** (different headline, colors, layout) linking to the same URL. Pinterest treats each design as fresh content.
- Best times (US audience): evenings 8–11pm ET and weekend mornings — but consistency beats timing.

## 4. Titles & descriptions (copy-paste templates)

**Pin title template:**
`[Keyword]: [Benefit] ([Year])` — e.g. "Best Resistance Bands for Home Workouts: 5 Picks Compared (2026)"

**Pin description template:**
```
[1 sentence on what the guide covers]. We compare [X, Y, Z] with honest pros and cons so you buy right the first time. [CTA: Tap to read the full guide + see current prices.]
#keyword1 #keyword2 #keyword3
```

**Example (resistance bands article):**
- Title: "Best Resistance Bands for Home Workouts (2026): 5 Sets Compared"
- Description: "Build a full home gym in a drawer. We compare stackable tube sets, loop bands, and fabric hip bands — resistance ranges, durability, and honest pros & cons. Tap to read the guide and check today's prices. #resistancebands #homeworkout #homegym #fitnessgear"

## 5. Linking pins to blog posts (the traffic engine)

1. Every pin links to the **article**, not Amazon. The article's product cards carry your affiliate tag.
2. **First comment on your own pin:** drop 1–2 extra keywords + "Full guide + prices at the link 🔗".
3. **Idea Pins / video pins:** 15–30s demo clips (unboxing, band exercises) with a link sticker to the article — video pins currently get extra distribution.
4. **Update pins seasonally:** "New Year home gym" (Jan), "Spring cleaning" (Mar–Apr), "Holiday gift guides" (Nov–Dec) — re-pin top articles with seasonal titles.

## 6. Rich Pins (do this once, benefit forever)

1. Apply for **Article Rich Pins**: add Open Graph tags (already in this site's `<head>`: `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`) — done.
2. Validate one article URL at Pinterest's Rich Pin Validator, then apply. Approval shows your headline + description automatically under every pin.
3. Keep `og:image` at 1200px+ (ours are 1200×630) so pins render large and sharp.

## 7. What to track (monthly, 15 minutes)

- **Outbound clicks** per pin (Pinterest Analytics → pin stats) — this is the money metric.
- **Top 3 articles by clicks** → make 2 more fresh pin designs for each.
- **Saves** signal algorithm love; **clicks** signal buyer intent. Optimize designs for clicks (clear CTA on image: "See prices →").
- Kill/refresh any pin design with <0.3% click-through after 30 days.

## 8. Compliance (don't get banned)

- **Disclose affiliate links** on the blog (we do — every article + /disclosure/). Pinterest doesn't require per-pin disclosure, but the landing page must have it (ours does).
- Don't cloak affiliate links with sketchy shorteners; direct Amazon links from the blog are fine.
- No "get rich quick" claims, no before/after body images, no medical cure claims (health pins) — Pinterest demotes all three.
