## Affiliate Disclosure

**DisplayJBZ is reader-supported.** When you buy through links on our site, we may earn an affiliate commission — at no extra cost to you.

### Amazon Associates

DisplayJBZ is a participant in the **Amazon Services LLC Associates Program**, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

That means: if you click an Amazon link on this site and make a purchase, Amazon pays us a small percentage of the sale. The price you pay is exactly the same as if you'd gone to Amazon directly.

### What this means for our recommendations

We want to be explicit, because trust is the whole business:

1. **Commissions never determine rankings.** We compare products on specs, value, and buyer-feedback patterns first. A product that pays a higher commission has never — and will never — outrank a better product that pays less.
2. **Every affiliate article carries a disclosure notice** at the top, so you always know the relationship exists before you read a single recommendation.
3. **Placeholders are labeled.** While the site is being built out, some product cards are clearly marked "Placeholder — replace before launch." We will never present a placeholder as a tested pick.
4. **No paid placements.** Brands cannot pay for a spot in our roundups, for a better rank, or for a positive write-up.

### Other affiliate programs

If we join additional affiliate programs in the future (beyond Amazon), this page will be updated and the same four rules will apply to them.
