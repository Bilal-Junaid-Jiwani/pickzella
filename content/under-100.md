## Why $100 is the sweet spot

Below $50, you are buying simplicity done well. Between $50 and $100, you start getting the features that make a product pleasant to live with: adjustable color temperature on a lamp, variable temperature on a kettle, denser foam on a roller. The guides above focus on that middle ground, where a little more money buys a lot more product.
