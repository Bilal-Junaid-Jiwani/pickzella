## Who we are

Pickzella started as a fitness-focused Pinterest page sharing workout ideas, home exercise tips, and gear that actually helps. As our community grew past 130,000 monthly viewers, one question kept coming up: **"which product should I actually buy?"**

This blog is our answer. We publish research-backed buying guides and product roundups across fitness, tech gadgets, home & kitchen, and everyday health, written in plain language, with honest pros and cons for every pick.

## How we research

Every guide on this site follows the same process:

1. **We define the use case first.** A "best" list is meaningless without knowing who it's for, beginners, small apartments, tight budgets, so each guide states its audience up front.
2. **We compare specifications side by side.** Dimensions, materials, battery life, warranties, the numbers that actually affect ownership.
3. **We read buyer feedback in bulk.** We look for patterns across hundreds of verified reviews: the same praise or complaint appearing again and again is signal; one-off rants are noise.
4. **We weigh value, not hype.** A product that does 90% of the job for 50% of the price usually beats the flagship.
5. **We list cons honestly.** If a popular product has a real weakness, you'll read it here, even if we link to it.

## How we make money (and why it doesn't corrupt the picks)

Pickzella participates in the Amazon Associates program. When you buy through a link on this site, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That's what keeps the guides free.

Two rules we never break:

- **Commissions never decide rankings.** A higher-commission product has never outranked a better-value one, and never will.
- **Placeholders are always labeled.** While we're building out the site, some product cards are marked as placeholders until final picks are confirmed. We'll never present a guess as a tested recommendation.

Read the full details on our [Affiliate Disclosure](/disclosure/) page.

## Editorial independence

No brand pays for placement, reviews, or rankings on Pickzella. If we ever accept a product for evaluation in the future, we'll say so clearly in the article.
