## A note on gifting tech

Tech gifts fail when they need setup the recipient will not do. Smart plugs win here: they pair in minutes and work with the voice assistant the person already has. Earbuds are the safe universal pick, and a good power bank is the gift everyone uses but nobody buys for themselves.
