## A note on gifting fitness gear

Fitness gifts fail when they assume a routine the person does not have yet. Stick to gear that works with zero commitment: a jump rope works in a driveway, bands work in a living room, and a water bottle works everywhere. Save the specialized equipment for when they tell you what they want next.
