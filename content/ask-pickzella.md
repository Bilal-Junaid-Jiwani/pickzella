faqs:
  - q: "Do brands pay you to recommend their products?"
    a: "No. We do not accept paid placements, and a brand cannot buy a Top Pick. Some links on the site are affiliate links, which means the retailer pays us a small commission if you buy through them. That commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your wallet, and it never changes which products we pick."
  - q: "How do you decide what to review?"
    a: "Every guide follows the same four steps. We define the use case, shortlist the most-bought options in the category, compare the specs that affect real ownership, read through buyer reviews for recurring praise and complaints, and score value as performance per dollar. Our suggestion box below is where most new guide ideas start."
  - q: "Do you test every product hands-on?"
    a: "We are honest about this: our process is research-driven, not a lab. We compare specifications, aggregate hundreds of verified buyer reviews, and look for patterns in what owners praise and complain about after months of use. We never claim hands-on testing we have not done."
  - q: "Why do the prices in your guides keep changing?"
    a: "Because retailers run deals constantly, especially on tech. We list the typical street price at the time of writing, and it is worth clicking through to check the current price before you buy. A pick that is $35 today and $49 next week is still the right pick, just a less exciting deal."
  - q: "How often do you update your guides?"
    a: "We revisit every guide regularly and rewrite it when the market moves: new models launch, prices shift permanently, or a pick gets discontinued. The last-updated date sits at the top of each guide so you can see how fresh it is."
  - q: "Why do you recommend budget picks instead of premium gear?"
    a: "Because value is performance per dollar, and the price-to-quality curve flattens fast in most categories. A $35 pair of earbuds is not 80 percent worse than a $180 pair. When premium genuinely earns its price, we say so and explain exactly what the extra money buys."
  - q: "What if I buy something you recommend and hate it?"
    a: "Buy from retailers with real return windows (Amazon's standard policy covers most of what we link) and keep the box until you are sure. If a pick has a widespread defect we missed, tell us. Reader reports are one of the fastest ways a guide gets corrected."
  - q: "Do your guides work outside the US?"
    a: "Our prices are in US dollars and our buying links point to US retailers like Amazon.com, which fits our core audience in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia. The buying criteria in each guide (what to prioritize, what to skip) apply anywhere. If a specific pick is not sold in your country, use the criteria to find its local equivalent."
  - q: "How do I compare two products that look identical on paper?"
    a: "Look past the spec sheet. Check the warranty length, the return policy, and what long-term owners complain about in reviews. Two products with the same specs often differ wildly in reliability, and that is where the real comparison happens."
  - q: "Can I suggest a product or a whole category for you to cover?"
    a: "Yes, and we genuinely want them. Use the suggestion box below this page. We plan future guides around reader requests, so the categories you ask for are the categories we research next."
