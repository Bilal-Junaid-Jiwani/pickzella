Print this page or tick the boxes as you shop. Each point comes from our [wireless earbuds guide](/posts/best-wireless-earbuds-under-50-2026/), which has the full reasoning.

- [ ] **Fit and tip sizes.** The box should include at least three tip sizes. Fit decides sound quality more than any spec; a bad seal means thin bass and buds that fall out.
- [ ] **Total battery over 30 hours.** Count buds plus case together. Buds alone run 5 to 8 hours; the case should push the total past 30.
- [ ] **Bluetooth 5.3 or newer.** Non-negotiable in 2026. Older versions mean more dropouts and worse call stability.
- [ ] **IPX5 water resistance if you exercise.** Sweat kills unprotected drivers in weeks. IPX4 handles splashes; no rating means desk use only.
- [ ] **Microphone expectations set.** Budget mics are fine indoors and rough in wind. If calls are your main use, pick a pair marketed for call clarity.
- [ ] **Controls you can live with.** Touch panels misfire when you adjust a bud; buttons click but push the bud deeper. Check whether gestures can be customized or disabled in the app.
- [ ] **Single-bud (mono) mode.** Either earbud should work alone while the other charges. Some cheap pairs only allow one side solo.
- [ ] **Low-latency mode for video.** Bluetooth lags behind video; a game or low-latency mode shrinks the gap. Ignore this if you only listen to music and podcasts.
- [ ] **Codec matched to your phone.** iPhone users want AAC; Android users get the most from LDAC or aptX where available. Fit and tuning still matter more than the codec.
- [ ] **A real return window.** Earbuds are personal: comfort reveals itself over days, not minutes. Buy from a seller with at least a 14-day return policy so a bad fit is not a sunk cost.
