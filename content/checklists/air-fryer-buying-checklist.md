Print this page or tick the boxes as you shop. Each point comes from our [air fryer guide](/posts/best-air-fryers-small-kitchens-2026/), which has the full reasoning.

- [ ] **Size matched to your household.** A 3 to 4.5-quart basket suits one or two people; go dual-basket or 8-quart only if you regularly cook for four or more. Bigger is not better; it is just bigger.
- [ ] **Counter and storage space measured.** Air fryers are bulky. Measure your counter depth and your cabinet shelf before buying, not after.
- [ ] **Basket over oven-style for most buyers.** Basket models cook more evenly and clean faster. Oven-style models with trays suit people who bake in theirs.
- [ ] **Temperature range up to 400°F.** That ceiling covers crisping, which is the whole point. A dehydrate function at the low end is a bonus, not a requirement.
- [ ] **Dishwasher-safe basket and crisper plate.** You will use this appliance more if cleanup takes two minutes. Check the manual, not just the listing, for which parts are dishwasher-safe.
- [ ] **Nonstick coating you are comfortable with.** Most baskets use a nonstick coating; ceramic options exist if you prefer to avoid PTFE. Either way, avoid metal utensils in the basket.
- [ ] **Controls you will actually use.** Presets are marketing; a simple temperature dial or a few clear buttons beat a crowded touchscreen. If it takes a manual to reheat fries, it is the wrong interface.
- [ ] **Noise level checked in reviews.** Air fryers are essentially small convection ovens with fans, and some are loud enough to notice across an open-plan room. Buyer reviews mention this more than listings do.
- [ ] **Spare parts available.** Brands that sell replacement baskets and crisper plates separately are telling you the appliance is meant to last. A cracked basket should not retire the whole unit.
- [ ] **Warranty and return window confirmed.** One year is the industry norm for the warranty; the return window is what protects you if the size is wrong. See our [warranty guide](/warranty-guide/) for what good coverage looks like.
