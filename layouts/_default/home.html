How we pick products

Every guide follows the same research process. No paid placements, ever.

  • Start with the use case. Who the guide is for, stated up front. No one-size-fits-all claims.
  • Compare the specs that matter. Side-by-side numbers that actually affect ownership and daily use.
  • Read buyers in bulk. Recurring praise and complaints across hundreds of reviews is signal.
  • Weigh value over hype. The pick that costs less and does the job usually wins.
